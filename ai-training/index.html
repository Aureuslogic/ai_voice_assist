Questions, answered plainly

What is corporate AI training? +

Corporate AI training teaches employees to use AI tools well and safely in their own roles. Great Business Platforms delivers it as practical workshops and programmes covering ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Microsoft Copilot.

Do staff need technical or coding skills? +

No. Our AI training needs no coding. It is written for marketing, HR, finance and other business teams, and starts from the tasks they already do.

Do you offer Microsoft Copilot training? +

Yes. We deliver Microsoft Copilot training for teams, alongside ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini training, including rules for using AI safely with company data.

Which teams do you train? +

We train marketing teams, HR teams and finance teams, as well as business owners and leadership teams who want a shared standard for using AI.

How do you train employees to use AI safely? +

Safe use is taught as a working habit. Staff learn what must never be pasted into an AI tool, how to check an answer, and who signs off AI-assisted work. We are registered with the ICO and work within UK GDPR.

Should we start with AI training or AI consultancy? +

Start with AI training if your processes are sound and adoption is the problem. Start with AI consultancy if work depends on manual handoffs or one or two key people. We will tell you which after a short conversation.
