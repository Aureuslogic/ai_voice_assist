TECHNICAL EXPLANATION Under 500ms End-to-End Latency

How Do AI Voice Agents Work?

An AI voice agent operates through four coordinated real-time systems that process the conversation in under 500 milliseconds:

01. STT / Speech-to-Text
Deepgram / Whisper
Transcribes incoming acoustic audio into text tokens in under 100ms.
02. Reasoning Engine
Gemini / Claude / GPT-4o
Interprets intent, checks system instructions, and crafts an immediate answer.
03. Real-Time TTS
ElevenLabs / VAPI
Streams lifelike human audio back to the caller without robotic delay.
04. Agentic Webhook
Make.com / CRM Sync
Simultaneously writes lead notes and calendar bookings directly to your CRM.
