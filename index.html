Why AI projects stall

AI projects stall long before the build starts.

We see the same four gaps in almost every stalled AI project. Close them first and the technology becomes the easy part.

01

No customer evidence

Teams solve the problem they assume exists, not the one customers actually complain about.

02

No cost of inaction

If the problem is never priced in hours and pounds, it never earns urgency or budget.

03

Wrong tool for the task

Months go into complex models for work a simple automation or a clear process could fix.

04

No agreed measure

Without a baseline set in advance, nobody can prove the return, and the next budget disappears.

How an AI voice agent works

  1. 01
    It listensSpeech-to-text turns the caller's words into text. Deepgram or Whisper.
  2. 02
    It thinksA language model reads intent, checks your rules and drafts a reply. Claude, Gemini or GPT.
  3. 03
    It speaksText-to-speech streams a natural voice back to the caller. ElevenLabs or VAPI.
  4. 04
    It actsDuring the call it books meetings and writes notes to your CRM. Make.com, HubSpot or GoHighLevel.
