AI Consultancy and AI Training: Your Questions, Answered Plainly.

What does Great Business Platforms do? +

Great Business Platforms delivers three specialist programmes for serious business owners, entrepreneurs, and teams: Business Workflow Development, Super Productivity in Marketing with AI, and Business Growth. We replace fragile, manual ways of working with calm, automated operational and commercial systems.

What is the difference between AI consultancy and AI training? +

AI consultancy means an outside expert designs, builds and deploys an AI solution for your business. AI training means your own team learns to use AI tools well and safely. Choose consultancy when the problem is a broken workflow or a lack of technical talent. Choose training when you have the tools and adoption is low. Great Business Platforms provides both.

Do we need AI consultancy or AI training first? +

Start with a short audit. If your processes and data are in good order, AI training gives the fastest return. If work depends on manual handoffs and one or two key people, fix the workflow with consultancy first, then train the team to run it. Most organisations need both, which is often called AI enablement or AI adoption.

What does an AI consultant do for a small or mid-sized business? +

An AI consultant finds where time and money leak, decides which tasks AI should handle, and builds the automation into the tools you already use. At Great Business Platforms that means four stages: Process Audit, System Design, Build and Deploy, and Team Enablement. You get a working system and a team that knows how to use it.

What is an AI readiness assessment? +

An AI readiness assessment is a structured review of your processes, data, tools and people that shows where AI will pay back and where it will not. Ours begins with the Process Audit: we map recurring tasks, approval delays and undocumented habits, then give you an AI implementation roadmap in priority order.

How can a business automate customer support and enquiries with AI? +

Begin with the enquiries that repeat. A Voice AI agent or AI assistant can answer common questions at any hour, AI email triage can sort and route messages, and your CRM can send status updates without manual chasing. People stay in charge of complaints, exceptions and anything that needs judgement.

What AI training do you offer for teams? +

We run role-specific AI training for marketing, HR and finance teams, covering ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Microsoft Copilot. Courses include Super Productivity in Marketing with AI, a seven-module programme, and Claude Training for Productivity. All of it is practical, covers prompt engineering for business, and needs no coding.

Do you offer Microsoft Copilot training? +

Yes. Great Business Platforms delivers Microsoft Copilot training for teams, alongside ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini training. Sessions are built around the work each team does, such as HR and finance, and include the rules for using AI safely with company data.

How do you train employees to use AI safely? +

Safe AI use is taught as a working habit. Staff learn what must never be pasted into an AI tool, how to check an answer before it is used, and who signs off AI-assisted work. Great Business Platforms is registered with the ICO, works within UK GDPR, and aligns its delivery with EU AI legislation and recognised best practice.

Where is Great Business Platforms based, and do you work across the UK? +

Great Business Platforms Ltd is a London-based AI consultancy and AI training provider, registered in England and Wales. We work with business owners and leadership teams across the United Kingdom, in person and remotely, and with clients internationally.

What is Business Workflow Development? +

It is a four-stage operational programme (Process Audit, System Design, Build & Deploy, and Team Enablement) where we map every recurring task in your organisation, connect your CRM and communication tools, and automate repetitive handoffs so your business runs smoothly without depending on heroic daily intervention.

What is Super Productivity in Marketing with AI, and does it require coding skills? +

None at all. It teaches marketing professionals and business owners to deploy ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Voice AI Agents across 7 structured modules to produce better marketing output faster, with zero coding required.
