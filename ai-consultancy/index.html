Questions, answered plainly

What does an AI consultancy do? +

An AI consultancy works out where AI will save a business time or money, then designs, builds and deploys the solution. Great Business Platforms does this in four stages: Process Audit, System Design, Build and Deploy, and Team Enablement.

How do we automate customer support with AI? +

Start with the questions customers ask most often. A Voice AI agent or AI assistant answers those at any hour, AI email triage sorts and routes the rest, and your CRM sends status updates. Your team handles complaints and exceptions.

Do you build custom AI assistants and voice agents? +

Yes. We build Voice AI agents and custom AI assistants around your own services, tone and data, and connect them to the tools you already use.

Is AI consultancy suitable for a small business? +

Yes. Small and mid-sized businesses often gain the most, because a few manual handoffs take a large share of the week. We begin with an audit so you only build what will pay back.

Do we need AI consultancy or AI training? +

Choose AI consultancy when workflows are broken or you lack technical talent in house. Choose AI training when you have the tools and adoption is low. Many businesses need both, in that order.

Where do you work? +

Great Business Platforms is based in London and works with businesses across the United Kingdom, in person and remotely, and with clients internationally.
